Privacy & data

This preview stores entries in this browser on this device. It does not sync across devices or automatically back up your data.

Do not enter bank account numbers, passwords, or other highly sensitive information. If you publish a shared tracker, anyone with access may be able to see its contents. For a public link, use fictional demo data or keep personal finance details private.
